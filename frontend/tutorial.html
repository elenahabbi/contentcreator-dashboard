Mini Tutorial

Five quick steps to get around ContentCreator. You don't need to be signed in to read this.

  1. Sign in and open your dashboard

    Choose Sign In on the welcome page. On the login form you can log in, or switch to Register to create an account. After signing in you land on your Dashboard.

    If your creator profile isn't set up yet, you'll first be asked for a display name and at least one content topic. The sidebar then takes you to the Dashboard, Content Planner, Scripts and Analytics pages.

  2. Write and manage scripts

    Open Scripts and choose Write Script. Give it a title, write the script, pick a type (Short Form, Long Form or Carousel) and a status (Draft, Filmed or Posted), then Save Script.

    Back on the Scripts page you can filter by status, open a script to read it, edit it or delete it.

  3. Schedule content and use the calendar

    In the Content Planner, choose + Schedule Event. Enter a title and a date, tick the platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), and optionally attach a script. Then Save.

    Click an item on the calendar to see its details. From there you can edit it, Mark Published once it's live, unpublish it, or delete it. The filter buttons show one platform at a time.

    A scheduled item whose date passes without being marked Published is counted as Missed.

  4. Review analytics and your posting habits

    The Analytics page has two separate posting measures:

    • Posting Goal Progress: choose Set posting goal to pick your own target (every day, a number of posts per week or per month, or a custom number and period). Progress compares the posts you actually published this period with that target. A goal is a target, not a schedule, so you don't have to schedule a post for every day.
    • Schedule Adherence: of the scheduled posts whose dates have passed, how many were published rather than missed.

    Performance numbers (views, likes, comments) and subscriber counts currently come from YouTube only. Connect your YouTube channel on the Analytics page. To link a video, mark its YouTube item as Published in the Content Planner, then use Find YouTube Video and Sync Analytics.

  5. Check opportunities and recommendations

    Your Dashboard can show Missed Opportunities and Recommendations. For each one, choose Mark Actioned once you've dealt with it, or Dismiss to clear it. The Creator Events section lets you add events that can feed into these suggestions.

    These lists only show something when the app has a suggestion for you, so they may be empty at first.
