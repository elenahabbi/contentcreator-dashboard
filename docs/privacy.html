# ContentCreator — Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 26 October 2026 ## 1. Introduction ContentCreator is an academic project developed as a web-based content planning and analytics dashboard for digital content creators. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, used and stored when the application is used. ## 2. Information the application may process Depending on the features enabled and the services connected, ContentCreator may process: - **Account information:** Basic profile information made available by an authorised social media platform, such as a platform user identifier and display name. - **Social media statistics:** Account-level statistics, such as follower counts, where the platform permits access. - **Public video information:** Information about publicly available videos, such as video titles, publication dates and available performance metrics. - **Content planning information:** Content ideas, scripts, categories, events and publishing schedules entered into the application. - **Authentication information:** Access tokens and related credentials needed to maintain an authorised connection to a third-party service. The information actually processed depends on the permissions granted and the features implemented. ## 3. How information is used Information may be used to: - Connect authorised social media accounts. - Retrieve permitted profile information, public videos and available statistics. - Organise content ideas, scripts and publishing schedules. - Display performance information and publishing consistency. - Identify potential content opportunities and provide recommendations. - Maintain and troubleshoot the application's functionality. ## 4. Storage and security Information may be stored in the application's database or processed by its backend. Authentication tokens and other sensitive credentials should be protected from unauthorised access and should not be exposed in public frontend code or public repositories. Reasonable security measures should be used to protect stored information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 5. Sharing of information ContentCreator does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to a connected platform when necessary to authenticate an account or retrieve information through that platform's authorised API. The application should not disclose private account information to unrelated third parties unless required for the service, authorised by the user or required by applicable law. ## 6. Third-party services Connecting a social media account is subject to that platform's authorisation process, permissions, privacy policy and terms. ContentCreator can access only the information made available through the permissions and services actually granted. Third-party services operate independently and have their own data practices. ## 7. Data retention and deletion Information may remain stored while needed for the application's functions, testing or project evaluation. Users should disconnect accounts they no longer wish to use and contact the project maintainer to request deletion of information associated with their use of the application. The application must implement the relevant deletion procedures before promising that all associated data can be deleted immediately. ## 8. Children's privacy ContentCreator is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit information about other people without an appropriate lawful basis or permission. ## 9. Changes to this policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as ContentCreator's features, integrations and data practices change. The latest version will be published on the official project website. ## 10. Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the project maintainer through the contact information provided on the official ContentCreator project website. --- ContentCreator is an academic demonstration project and is not an official TikTok product.
